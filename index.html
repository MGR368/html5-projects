Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

This recipe makes crispy yet chewy cookies. Best served warm with cold milk!

Prep Time: 15 mins | Bake Time: 10 mins | Yield: 12 cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter (softened)
  • ¾ cup white sugar
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 ¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a large bowl, mix butter and sugars until smooth.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla extract; mix well.
  4. Gradually stir in the flour mixture.
    Note: Do not overmix the dough!
  5. Fold in chocolate chips.
  6. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes or until golden brown.
